# personas/ — persona AIOS dirs (two kinds, both door-emitted)

**Rolled-up persona AIOSes** (`chainaios.roll_up_algebra`, `persona_root=`): a `CLAUDE.md`
(the persona — a `PersonaSpec` CoR seated over its minted AC→CoR→SC algebra in `../skills/`),
a `legend.json` (the GlyphSteer GRADE vocabulary), and a `kb/`.

**GBA AIOSes** (`chainaios.make_gba` + `construct_into`) — e.g. `systemic-archive-narrator/`:
a full persistent BanditAgent dir the agent GOES TO AND BECOMES — persona `CLAUDE.md` (the
melt-gauge), `.claude/rules/` (loop · law · KB-reward · self-expansion), a live coordinate-
addressed SkillTree under `.claude/skills/` (the minted AC/CoR/SC systems, each sidechain
with its OWN persona seat), `kb/`, `gba.json` (the manifest), and a rebuildable `.index.db`
(gitignored). The gated bodies live in the sibling `.<name>.src/` scratch, which PERSISTS
(the manifest's `skill_src` entries point there — do not delete it).

Go to a subdir and run an agent there to BECOME that persona. Generated through the door —
do not hand-edit emitted dirs; re-mint (`roll_up_algebra`) or `construct_into` instead.
