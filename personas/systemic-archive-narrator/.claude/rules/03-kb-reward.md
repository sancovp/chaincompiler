# Rule: the KB reward record

Keep a knowledge base under `kb/`: one note per topic, each headed with a grade (🏆 ✅ ⚠️ ❌) = the reward. On a new task, surface your best-graded prior chains first (that is `Recall`). When a chain earns 🏆 twice, file a goldenization request (`goldenreq`) with the graded notes as evidence — it becomes golden only on an accepted verdict, never by itself; demote ❌ out of Recall.
